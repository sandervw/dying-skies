### 1. Glass Pluck / Mallet Lead (Arpeggiated Theme)
* **Role:** Pluck / Bell
* **Tone.js Type:** `Tone.FMSynth` (or `Tone.PolySynth(Tone.FMSynth)`)
* **Register / Note Range:** High (C5 – C7)
* **Oscillator Configuration:**
  * Type: `sine` (Carrier), `sine` (Modulator)
  * Polyphony / Voicing: Polyphonic (4–6 voices)
  * Harmonicity / Modulation Index: `harmonicity: 3.5`, `modulationIndex: 8.0`
* **Amplitude Envelope (ADSR):**
  * Attack: `0.005s`
  * Decay: `0.6s`
  * Sustain: `0.1`
  * Release: `0.8s`
* **Filter Envelope & Cutoff:**
  * Filter Type: `lowpass` (12 dB/oct)
  * Base Cutoff: `3500Hz`
  * Envelope Amount / Octaves: `2.5`
  * Attack: `0.005s` | Decay: `0.4s` | Sustain: `0.2` | Release: `0.6s`
* **Brightness & Timbre:** Bright, crystalline, glassy, delicate mallet-like strike
* **Movement & Modulation:**
  * Pitch Mod / Vibrato: `rate: 4.5Hz`, `depth: 0.05`
  * Detune / Unison: `spread: 10 cents`
  * LFO Destinations: Stereo Panning (`rate: 0.2Hz`, `min: -0.4`, `max: 0.4`)
* **FX Chain (Ordered signal flow):**
  1. `Tone.Chorus` (`frequency: 1.5`, `delayTime: 3.5`, `depth: 0.4`, `wet: 0.35`)
  2. `Tone.FeedbackDelay` (`delayTime: "8n."`, `feedback: 0.45`, `wet: 0.35`)
  3. `Tone.Reverb` (`decay: 3.8`, `preDelay: 0.03`, `wet: 0.45`)

### 2. Ethereal Dream Pad
* **Role:** Pad
* **Tone.js Type:** `Tone.PolySynth(Tone.Synth)`
* **Register / Note Range:** Mid to High (C3 – G5)
* **Oscillator Configuration:**
  * Type: `fatsawtooth`
  * Polyphony / Voicing: Polyphonic (6 voices)
* **Amplitude Envelope (ADSR):**
  * Attack: `1.8s`
  * Decay: `2.0s`
  * Sustain: `0.85`
  * Release: `3.0s`
* **Filter Envelope & Cutoff:**
  * Filter Type: `lowpass` (24 dB/oct)
  * Base Cutoff: `1200Hz`
  * Envelope Amount / Octaves: `1.2`
  * Attack: `2.0s` | Decay: `2.5s` | Sustain: `0.7` | Release: `3.0s`
* **Brightness & Timbre:** Warm, lush, airy, submerged
* **Movement & Modulation:**
  * Detune / Unison: `count: 3`, `spread: 20 cents`
  * LFO Destinations: Filter Cutoff (`type: sine`, `rate: 0.08Hz`, `min: 800Hz`, `max: 1800Hz`)
* **FX Chain (Ordered signal flow):**
  1. `Tone.Chorus` (`frequency: 0.8`, `delayTime: 4.0`, `depth: 0.7`, `wet: 0.5`)
  2. `Tone.Reverb` (`decay: 6.0`, `preDelay: 0.08`, `wet: 0.65`)

* **Tone.js Type:** `Tone.MonoSynth`

* **Oscillator Configuration:**

* **Amplitude Envelope (ADSR):**
  * Attack: `0.8s`
  * Decay: `1.0s`
  * Sustain: `1.0`
  * Release: `2.0s`
* **Filter Envelope & Cutoff:**
  * Filter Type: `lowpass` (24 dB/oct)
  * Base Cutoff: `220Hz`
  * Envelope Amount / Octaves: `0.5`
  * Attack: `0.8s` | Decay: `1.0s` | Sustain: `1.0` | Release: `2.0s`
* **Brightness & Timbre:** Dark, smooth, deep, grounding
* **Movement & Modulation:**
