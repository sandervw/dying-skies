### 1. Warm Synth Brass Pad / Drone
* **Role:** Drone / Pad
* **Tone.js Type:** `Tone.PolySynth(Tone.Synth)`
* **Register / Note Range:** Low to Mid (`F2` – `A4`)
* **Oscillator Configuration:**
  * Type: `fatsawtooth`
  * Polyphony / Voicing: Polyphonic (4–6 voices)
* **Amplitude Envelope (ADSR):**
  * Attack: `0.35s`
  * Decay: `1.2s`
  * Sustain: `0.75`
  * Release: `1.5s`
* **Filter Envelope & Cutoff:**
  * Filter Type: `lowpass` (24 dB/oct)
  * Base Cutoff: `450Hz`
  * Envelope Amount / Octaves: `2.5`
  * Attack: `0.4s` | Decay: `1.0s` | Sustain: `0.6` | Release: `1.5s`
* **Brightness & Timbre:** Warm, analog brass-like, mellow yet rich in lower-mid harmonics
* **Movement & Modulation:**
  * Pitch Mod / Vibrato: subtle (`rate: 4.5Hz`, `depth: 0.03`)
  * Detune / Unison: `spread: 18 cents`, `count: 3`
* **FX Chain:**
  1. `Tone.Chorus` (`frequency: 1.2`, `delayTime: 3.5`, `depth: 0.6`, `wet: 0.35`)
  2. `Tone.Reverb` (`decay: 3.5s`, `preDelay: 0.02s`, `wet: 0.45`)

* **Oscillator Configuration:**

* **Amplitude Envelope (ADSR):**
  * Attack: `0.005s`
  * Decay: `0.7s`
  * Sustain: `0.1`
  * Release: `0.9s`
* **Filter Envelope & Cutoff:**
  * Filter Type: `lowpass` (12 dB/oct)
  * Base Cutoff: `3800Hz`
  * Envelope Amount / Octaves: `1.5`
* **Brightness & Timbre:** Bright, crystalline, glassy, delicate transient
* **Movement & Modulation:**
  * Detune: subtle drift (`spread: 6 cents`)
* **FX Chain:**
  1. `Tone.PingPongDelay` (`delayTime: "8n."`, `feedback: 0.35`, `wet: 0.3`)
  2. `Tone.Reverb` (`decay: 4.0s`, `wet: 0.5`)

* **Register / Note Range:** Mid-High transient (`C4` – `A4`)
* **Oscillator Configuration:**
  * Type: `triangle`
  * Polyphony / Voicing: Monophonic
* **Amplitude Envelope (ADSR):**
  * Attack: `0.001s`
  * Decay: `0.08s`
  * Sustain: `0.0`
  * Release: `0.08s`
* **Filter Envelope & Cutoff:**
  * Filter Type: `bandpass` (12 dB/oct)
  * Base Cutoff: `1200Hz`
  * Q: `2.0`
* **Brightness & Timbre:** Hollow, woody, dry, percussive tick
* **Movement & Modulation:**
  * Pitch Mod / Envelope: fast drop (`octaves: 3`, `pitchDecay: 0.02s`)
