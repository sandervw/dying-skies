* **Movement & Modulation:**
  * Pitch Mod / Vibrato: None
  * Detune / Unison: `spread: 0 cents, count: 1`
  * LFO Destinations: None
  * Portamento / Glide: `0.0s`

### 2. Acoustic Classical Guitar (Arpeggio & Treble Pluck)
* **Role:** Pluck / Lead
* **Tone.js Type:** `Tone.PolySynth(Tone.FMSynth)`
* **Register / Note Range:** Mid to High (C4 – E6)
* **Oscillator Configuration:**
  * Type: `sine` (Carrier), `triangle` (Modulator)
  * Polyphony / Voicing: Polyphonic (6 voices)
  * Harmonicity / Modulation Index (FM/AM only): `harmonicity: 2.0, modulationIndex: 1.8`
* **Amplitude Envelope (ADSR):**
  * Attack: `0.002s`
  * Decay: `0.9s`
  * Sustain: `0.02`
  * Release: `0.5s`
* **Filter Envelope & Cutoff (Optional/MonoSynth):**
  * Filter Type: `lowpass` (24 dB/oct)
  * Base Cutoff: `2800Hz`
  * Envelope Amount / Octaves: `2.2`
  * Attack: `0.002s` | Decay: `0.4s` | Sustain: `0.1` | Release: `0.4s`
* **Brightness & Timbre:** Crisp, organic, bright acoustic nylon chime, transient string-nail snap
* **Movement & Modulation:**
  * Pitch Mod / Vibrato: subtle expressive vibrato on sustained notes (`rate: 5.5Hz, depth: 0.015`)
  * Detune / Unison: `spread: 4 cents, count: 2`
  * LFO Destinations: None
  * Portamento / Glide: `0.0s`
* **FX Chain (Ordered signal flow):**
  1. `Tone.Compressor` (`threshold: -18, ratio: 3, attack: 0.01, release: 0.1`)
  2. `Tone.EQ3` (`low: -2.0, mid: 1.5, high: 2.0`)
  3. `Tone.Reverb` (`decay: 2.2, preDelay: 0.015, wet: 0.3`)
